# quantum-hardware-experiments
Explorations in quantum hardware design using open-source tools such as Qiskit and Qiskit Metal, documenting experiments, notes, and small prototypes connecting semiconductor design concepts with emerging quantum systems.
This repository documents my personal exploration of quantum hardware systems, particularly superconducting qubit circuit design and open-source tools used for quantum device development.

My background is in semiconductor physical design (EDA tools and ASIC implementation). I am interested in understanding how classical chip design methodologies intersect with emerging quantum hardware architectures.

This repository contains:

• small experiments using Qiskit and Qiskit Metal
• notes from reading research papers on quantum hardware
• exploratory notebooks related to qubit circuit design

The goal is to build intuition around quantum hardware design and contribute to open-source tooling in this area.
